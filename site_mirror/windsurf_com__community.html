Page: https://windsurf.com/community

Summary

This is the community engagement section of the Windsurf website, where visitors can connect with other Windsurf users through various platforms. The page offers three main ways to engage: joining the Discord server to connect with over 100k users, participating in the Reddit community, and checking the events calendar to stay informed about in-person events. Below this section, visitors can also download the Windsurf Editor for Windows by selecting their processor architecture (x64 or arm64), with additional download options available for other operating systems.
