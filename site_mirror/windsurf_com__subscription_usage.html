Page: https://windsurf.com/subscription/usage

Summary

This is a Windsurf account usage dashboard page where users can monitor their subscription and credit usage. The page displays information about a free trial period, shows the number of user prompt credits and add-on prompt credits available and used, and provides options to upgrade the plan or purchase additional credits. Users can also access other account management features through the left sidebar menu, including profile settings, notifications, plan management, and referrals.
