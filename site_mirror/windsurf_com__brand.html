Page: https://windsurf.com/brand

Summary

This is Windsurf's Brand Guidelines page, which provides instructions on the proper use of the Windsurf logo and brand assets to maintain brand consistency. Visitors can view detailed guidelines about logo usage including dos and don'ts (such as not outlining the logo, not applying effects, maintaining proper spacing, etc.), see logo variations for different backgrounds, and download official brand assets through a dedicated button. The page serves as a resource for partners, media, or anyone who needs to use Windsurf's branding materials correctly.
