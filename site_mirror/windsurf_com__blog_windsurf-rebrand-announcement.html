Page: https://windsurf.com/blog/windsurf-rebrand-announcement

Summary

This is a blog post announcing that the company formerly known as Codeium is officially rebranding to Windsurf. The article explains the company's evolution from Exafunction to Codeium and now to Windsurf, highlighting how their flagship AI-assisted coding product, the Windsurf Editor, represents a turning point that led to this broader company rebranding. Visitors can read about the company's future plans, including continuing development of the Editor, rebranding existing Codeium Extensions to Windsurf Plugins, and introducing new products in the Windsurf ecosystem, and they can also subscribe to the blog or download the product.
