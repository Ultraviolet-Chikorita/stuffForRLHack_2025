Page: https://windsurf.com/subscription/manage-plan

Summary

This is the Plan Management page of Windsurf's account settings, where users can view and modify their subscription details. Visitors can see their current plan status (currently on a free trial), upgrade their subscription, update payment information and billing address, view past invoices, or cancel their plan while retaining access until the end of their billing period.
